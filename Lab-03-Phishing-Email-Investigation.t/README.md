
# Lab 03 - Phishing Email Investigation

## Objective

Analyze a simulated phishing email and identify indicators that could suggest malicious intent.

## Environment

- Simulated phishing email sample
- Manual header and content review
- IOC extraction workflow

## Tasks Performed

- Reviewed sender and reply-to information
- Identified sender/reply-to mismatch
- Examined suspicious domains and links
- Reviewed urgency and account suspension language
- Checked email authentication indicators
- Identified mock SPF, DKIM, and DMARC results
- Extracted indicators of compromise
- Mapped the phishing method to MITRE ATT&CK technique T1566.002

## Key Findings

The simulated email contained several phishing indicators, including:

- A suspicious sender domain
- Reply-to mismatch
- Urgent language
- Account suspension pressure
- Suspicious links
- Failed or missing email authentication controls

The scenario was designed for training and was not a real malicious email received by the user.

## Skills Demonstrated

- Phishing email triage
- Sender analysis
- Header analysis
- IOC identification
- Email authentication review
- MITRE ATT&CK mapping
- SOC analyst decision-making
